# 📰 BugBaar AI Newsletter

> **An open-source newsletter delivering curated insights on Artificial Intelligence, Engineering, Startups, Careers, and Open Source.**

The BugBaar AI Newsletter is a community-driven publication that helps developers, founders, students, and builders stay informed about the technologies shaping the future.

Our mission is simple:

**Deliver signal over noise.**

---

# 🤔 Why BugBaar AI Newsletter?

The technology landscape changes every day.

Thousands of articles, research papers, product launches, and AI announcements are published daily.

Most people don't have time to keep up.

The BugBaar AI Newsletter filters the noise and delivers only what truly matters.
